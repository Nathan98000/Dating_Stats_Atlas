# Phase 5 H: card photo candidates (for Nathan to pick)

Twelve metros (the top 10 of either reference search, 8 Oct 2026). All twelve qualify: eleven current photos are CC BY / BY-SA (not public domain or CC0), and Portland's is CC0 but a drone aerial. Up to three candidates each, found on Wikimedia Commons. **Every candidate is public domain or CC0 and passes the pipeline's licence gate** (`city_images.clear_licence` + the review's refused list), so no CC BY fallback was needed. Each was checked by eye at 1600 px for street level, daylight, close faces, sculpture/mural subjects and government emblems. Local copies (Wikimedia's own 1600 px rendition, gitignored) are in `results/phase5/_photos/<slug>_<n>.jpg`. Full records: `photo_candidates.json`.

Nothing ships until picked: no render data, review record or manifest was changed.

## Notes and open points

- **No metro lacks a PD/CC0 street-level candidate.** The thinnest are Seattle (Commons' Seattle street photos are mostly CC BY-SA; candidate 2 is shot from about one storey up) and New Orleans (older or overcast shots; no strong sunny French Quarter street in PD/CC0 that passed the people/emblem checks).
- **Faces to review:** Boston 2 (a woman crossing at the right edge) and Chicago 1 (a man near centre at mid-distance) show faces at small size; neither is the subject, but a cover-crop could bring them forward.
- **Unverified place:** Chicago 2's Commons description (a 500px import) does not name the street. Austin 1's description says only 'Austin, Texas' (the scene is downtown).
- **Rejected after viewing:** Austin 'P01 Austin, Texas 05' (a woman mid-frame facing the camera), Philadelphia 'South Street' (a possible Pennsylvania state flag mid-frame), New Orleans 'French Quarter Street' Oct 2024 (an unidentified large flag, a mid-distance crowd).
- Unsplash-sourced files (several Boston, New York, Seattle candidates) carry CC0 on their Commons pages (Unsplash photos uploaded before its June 2017 licence change were CC0); the gate reads that Commons field. Bernard Spragg's Powell Street file is recorded on Commons as public domain.
