# Terms of use

**In short:** Dating Stats Atlas shows estimates made from public survey
data for your general interest. Enjoy it and share it, but don't treat a
figure as a fact about an individual, and never use the site to judge or
single anyone out.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

## What the site is

For each US metro area, the site estimates how many single people match
your search, how closely they resemble the people who typically pair up
with someone like you, and what everyday life there is like. It is not a
dating service: there are no accounts, profiles, messages or
introductions.

## Estimates, not recommendations

- The counts come from the Census Bureau's household survey, which
  samples households, so every figure carries some uncertainty. Where the
  sample is too thin to trust, the site leaves the city out rather than
  show a number; the about page explains how the numbers are made.
- The numbers are Dating Stats Atlas's own estimates from Census Bureau
  and other public data. No government agency made, checked or endorses
  them.
- We work to keep the figures accurate, but we can't promise they are
  complete, current or right for your situation, and none of them is
  advice.

## Using the site

You're welcome to browse, search, compare cities and share links to what
you find, and to quote a few figures with a link back to the page they
came from. Please don't:

- use the site, or anything from it, to make a decision about someone
  else's housing, credit, job, insurance or education, or to single out,
  target or treat anyone differently because of their race, ethnicity,
  sex or any other personal trait;
- copy the site's figures in bulk, scrape the site, collect its data
  automatically, or reach the service behind it any way other than
  through its pages;
- present the site's figures as official statistics, or suggest that we
  or any government agency endorse you or what you offer;
- try to break, overload or get around the site's security or its
  limits;
- use the site in any way that breaks the law.
