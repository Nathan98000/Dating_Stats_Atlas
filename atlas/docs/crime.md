# About the crime figures

Crime numbers look simple and are not. This page says what the figures on
city pages are, what they are not, and why this site never scores or
ranks cities by them.

## What the numbers are

Each city page can show two figures, both from the FBI's national
crime-reporting program:

- **Violent crime** — murder, rape, robbery and aggravated assault
  reported to police, per 100,000 people a year.
- **Property crime** — burglary, larceny-theft and motor vehicle theft
  reported to police, per 100,000 people a year.

They count crimes **reported to police agencies that submitted a full
year of data**, added up across the metro area's agencies. Crimes that
never get reported aren't in them, and reporting habits differ from place
to place.

## What "coverage" means

Police agencies report to the FBI voluntarily, and in any given year some
of a metro's agencies report every month, some report a few months, and
some don't report at all. **Coverage** is the share of a metro's people
living in areas whose agencies reported a full year.

The rates divide reported crimes **only by the population those agencies
cover** — never by the whole metro — so a city where half the agencies
went quiet doesn't get a rate that looks artificially low. When coverage
falls below the bar this site requires, no figure is shown at all: a
number built from a sliver of a metro isn't one we'd stand behind.

## Why cities aren't compared on crime

Two cities' crime figures are made by different sets of agencies, with
different reporting habits, covering different shares of their people.
That's the FBI's own position: it cautions against using this data to
rank or compare places, and this site follows it. Crime appears on city
pages and the compare page as context, is never part of any score, and
has no "cities by crime" list — that page doesn't exist here on purpose.

## Where it comes from

FBI Crime Data Explorer, agency-level submissions, aggregated to metro
areas using the government's own county definitions. The reference year
is shown with the figures.
